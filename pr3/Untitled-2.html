Space & Astronomy

Did you know that a day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus? It takes Venus longer to rotate once on its axis than to complete one orbit of the Sun.

Ancient History

Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. Teaching existed at Oxford as early as 1096, whereas the Aztec civilization began around 1325.

Nature & Science

Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs that are over 3,000 years old and still completely edible.
